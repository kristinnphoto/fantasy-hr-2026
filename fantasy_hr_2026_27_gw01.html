The Haaland problem

Effective ownership 138%
2 Points scored by Erling Haaland

Ten armbands, four points

Twelve of sixteen managers owned him. Ten of them captained him. He returned two points, which means the captaincy paid out four — and every one of those ten started the season already behind the six who didn't.

10 / 16 captained Haaland — the other six went Bruno, Thiago, Isak, Semenyo, Wirtz and João Pedro.

Only one of those six was right. Ólafur's Platelet FC handed the armband to João Pedro, banked 22 points from one player, and sits fifth on a squad that scored 57 with no chip at all.
